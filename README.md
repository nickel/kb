## Finances

### Basic

- ECL -> Book a loss reserve today for the slice of that money you statistically expect to lose. [[ECL|More info]].
- EIR -> The rate that exactly discounts contractual future cash flows to the asset's gross carrying amount. [[eir|More info]].
- Interest Accrual -> Recognition of interest income as revenue, per installment, on its due date — not at origination, not on the day batch runs. [[interest-accrual|More info]].
- Income Recognition -> IFRS 9 basis converts a loan's contractual cost into revenue over time, and therefore which ledger path the loan takes. [[income-recognition|More info]].
