# 2026-09-18


We have worked mainly on three things:

* Adjusting the posting in the ledgers, with the daily posting of interest accrual as the main change.
* Adjusting the batch export for the ERP.
* And mainly, pushing forward a lot of the decisions we have open. There is one about income recognition that is giving us quite a lot of work, but it is only a matter of deciding, both options are already implemented.

---
The big change this time is interest. It now builds up daily in the books, and the IFRS 9 interest calculations are fixed. That means a month end now shows the interest earned so far, not only what was due. The effective interest amount per period is correct as well, and it stays correct after payment holidays and term changes.

We also cleaned up what happens when a loan closes. An early repayment books the quoted amount, and withdrawn loans no longer count interest twice. In the demo book that was 1,292.71 SEK across 8 loans. On top of that, when a loan leaves the books, the leftover late fees and deferred fees are cleared too.

On the product side, the signed IFRS 9 classification for the personal loan is now recorded, so the product can be set up And operators have something new in the back office: they can see a loan ledger as T accounts and download it to Excel.

The Swedish accounting export also changed shape. SIE is now one file per day, with one entry per type of event instead of one per loan. To give you an idea, a busy day is 75 KB, while the old full year file was 4.2 MB.

For supervisory reporting we fixed three things in FINREP. Profit and loss is year to date, so a closed month no longer reports zero income. Currencies are no longer added together. And the deposit system figures are no longer filed as zero.

Last one is VAT. A market with no VAT rate set up now fails instead of silently charging 0%, and the rate we apply is the gone valid on the date of the sale.

---

- Swedish statutory export (SIE) — the biggest piece. Built end to end: read the whole book, convert it to the Swedish standard accounting-file format, and let an operator download it from the backoffice. Along the way:
    - Restricted it to the entities that actually report in Sweden, so a branch can't produce a filing it has no standing to make.
    - Closed three ways an export could have been forged or rejected by the receiving system, and made the file signed by the platform rather than by whoever clicked download.
    - Made bad characters report where they are instead of failing opaquely.
    - Corrected what the file claims to be, and separated three kinds of closing entry that had been conflated.
    - Switched from one file per fiscal year to one file per day (with the statutory account chart still anchored to the year).
    - Aggregated entries so each day produces one verification per transaction type instead of one per event.
    - Filed the two open questions the build had been answering silently.
- Daily interest accrual
    - Interest now books every day instead of in a lump on each installment date, so a month-end close shows interest actually earned. Period totals unchanged. Then three rounds of independent review plus two follow-on fixes: a rescheduled installment now hands its replacement the right calendar, and the loan is locked before checking whether interest was already booked, so two processes can't double-book.
- Regulatory reporting (FINREP)
    - Profit or loss now reported year-to-date rather than cumulatively — it was overstating.
    - Each reported figure now declares which system's books it comes from.
    - A report run against a future period that already has a balance is now refused instead of producing a wrong number.
    - Two deposit rows corrected at the source rather than patched in demo data.
- Ledger correctness — roughly a dozen fixes. The recurring theme is money that was being counted twice, dropped, or dated wrong:
    - Early withdrawal was recognizing interest twice.
    - Late-fee receivables weren't cleared when a loan left the book.
    - VAT was resolving at the wrong date instead of the date of the supply.
    - Settlement add-backs were spanning periods they shouldn't.
    - The end-of-loan residual check only covered some exits; now covers all five.
    - Month-end close is now blocked unless the month's settlements tally.
    - Per-entry balances are re-derived daily rather than trusted.
    - Plus internal hardening: failures inside transactions can no longer be swallowed, and the close-evidence checks are pinned so they can't be pointed at the wrong data.
- Product and housekeeping
    - Recorded the signed IFRS 9 classification for the personal loan product (the accounting treatment Finance signs off), and stated the statutory fee reduction in that assessment. Plus demo reseed fix, seed reporting fix, and removing a stale counts footer from the architecture map.

Open: FIN-6 (the daily accrual policy) is still unsigned — the code follows what the CFO confirmed on the worksheets, not a decision on record.
